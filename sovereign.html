What You Should Know.

What is Genesis Sovereign?

Sovereign is the highest expression of Genesis Telemed Plus, offering our greatest level of access, in-home care, and personal coordination.

.

Can I reach Genesis at any time?

Yes. Sovereign provides 24/7 access to a Genesis provider.

.

Can Genesis come to my home?

Yes. Private house calls are available within the Miami area, subject to the terms of your membership.

.

What does personal concierge include?

Genesis can assist with referrals, scheduling, and coordination when your care extends beyond our team.

Can Genesis accompany me to another physician?

Yes. When appropriate, Sovereign can include accompaniment to outside medical appointments, helping bring greater continuity to your care.

How is Sovereign different from Master?

Sovereign offers Genesis's highest degree of access and personal involvement, with expanded in-home availability and deeper coordination beyond Genesis.

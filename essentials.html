Membership Questions

What You Should Know.

.

What is Genesis Essentials?

Essentials is the starting point of the Genesis experience, offering preferred access to our team alongside functional medicine and health optimization.

.

Where is Essentials available?

Essentials is delivered remotely, allowing you to connect with Genesis from wherever you are, subject to applicable medical requirements.

.

What areas can Genesis support?

Depending on your individual needs, your care may involve functional medicine, metabolic health, hormone health, peptides, laboratory insights, wearables, and broader health optimization.

.

Can my family join Genesis?

Yes. Household options are available for those who wish to bring family members into Genesis.

.

What if I want greater access?

Genesis offers elevated levels for those who want greater availability, in-person care, and a more extensive concierge experience.

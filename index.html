Before You Begin

Membership
Questions

Everything you need to know before you begin.

Reach the concierge line
I

What is Zennia Concierge?

Zennia Concierge is a private medical membership centered on your vitality, performance, and longevity, with personalized care and exceptional access to your medical team.

II

What does membership give me access to?

Your level of access depends on your membership, ranging from personalized physician care and ongoing health optimization to extended provider availability, concierge support, and select in-home care.

III

Can I reach my care team after hours?

Yes. Select memberships provide extended or around-the-clock provider access, so care isn’t limited to traditional office hours.

IV

What if I just have a simple health question?

Ask us. From medications and injections to symptoms or everyday health concerns, your Zennia team is there for the questions that matter to you.

V

Can Zennia come to my home?

Select memberships include access to eligible in-home care in Miami, including physician visits and certain medical and wellness services.

VI

Can my family join Zennia?

Yes. Household membership options are available, allowing you to extend the Zennia experience to the people closest to you.

VII

What happens when I need care outside of Zennia?

Depending on your membership, our concierge team can assist with referrals, scheduling, and coordination to help make the experience as seamless as possible.

VIII

Can I use Zennia while traveling?

Your relationship with Zennia isn’t confined to a physical office. Virtual access allows you to stay connected with your care team while traveling, subject to applicable medical and licensing requirements.

IX

How private is my membership?

Privacy and discretion are fundamental to the Zennia experience. Your medical information is handled in accordance with applicable healthcare privacy and security requirements.

X

How do I become a member?

Begin by connecting with Zennia. We’ll help you select the level of membership and access that best fits your needs and lifestyle.
